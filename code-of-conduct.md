Bioinformatics Hub is a space for learning. Many members are new to the field, posting in
a second or third language, and asking questions in public for the first time. The
behaviour below keeps that possible.

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Assume that a question asked in good faith deserves an answer in good faith.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Credit people's work, data and code.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Dismissing or belittling beginners' questions.
- Sharing identifiable patient or participant data, or any data you are not licensed to share.
- Spam, unsolicited advertising or recruitment unrelated to bioinformatics.

## Scope

This code applies in the Slack workspace, in this GitHub repository and its issues and
pull requests, at hub events, and whenever an individual is representing the community
in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the steering committee by direct message on Slack, or by
email to the contact address on the [members page]({{ '/members/' | relative_url }}).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is
deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The team is obliged to maintain
confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the steering committee.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that are not aligned to this code of conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviours they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful.
