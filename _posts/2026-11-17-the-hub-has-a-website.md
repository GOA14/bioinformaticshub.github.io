The Bioinformatics Hub Slack workspace has been running since 2016 as a place for
scientists and researchers in Nigeria to talk about bioinformatics. It now has a public
home as well.

**What is new.** A [members page]({{ '/members/' | relative_url }}) you add yourself to
through GitHub, an [events calendar]({{ '/events/' | relative_url }}), a
[resources page]({{ '/resources/' | relative_url }}) of starting points for people new to
the field, and this blog. Everything is held in a public repository, so any member can
correct a mistake or add a page with a pull request.

**Why GitHub.** Membership lives in one plain-text file,
[`_data/members.yml`](https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}/blob/main/_data/members.yml).
Open the [membership form](https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}/issues/new?template=membership.yml),
answer three required questions, and a bot writes your entry and opens a pull request.
Your GitHub username and profile photo come from the account you post with, so the list
cannot be filled in on your behalf. Nobody has to maintain a database, and you keep
control of your own entry.

**What we need.** Volunteers to serve as administrators from Nigerian institutions,
people willing to host an online meetup, and anyone who wants to write up a tutorial for
the blog. If your institution's email domain is not yet accepted by the Slack workspace,
tell us and we will add it.

Start here: [how to join]({{ '/join/' | relative_url }}).
