## Learning the basics

- [Data Carpentry: Genomics](https://datacarpentry.org/genomics-workshop/) &mdash; shell, cloud and variant-calling lessons for complete beginners.
- [Software Carpentry: The Unix Shell](https://swcarpentry.github.io/shell-novice/) &mdash; the single most useful thing to learn first.
- [Galaxy Training Network](https://training.galaxyproject.org/) &mdash; hundreds of hands-on tutorials, no command line required.
- [Bioconductor course material](https://bioconductor.org/help/course-materials/) &mdash; R workflows for genomics.
- [Rosalind](https://rosalind.info/) &mdash; bioinformatics by problem solving.

## African and Nigerian networks

- [African Bioinformatics Institute (ABI)](https://bioinformaticsinstitute.africa/) &mdash; continent-wide network building bioinformatics research, training and infrastructure across Africa.
- [African BioGenome Project (AfricaBP)](https://africanbiogenome.org/) &mdash; pan-African effort to sequence African biodiversity, with an Open Institute running genomics and bioinformatics training.
- [H3ABioNet](https://www.h3abionet.org/) &mdash; pan-African bioinformatics network, accredited training and node support.
- [ASBCB](https://www.asbcb.org/) &mdash; African Society for Bioinformatics and Computational Biology.
- [Science Communication Hub Nigeria](https://scicomnigeria.org/) &mdash; mentorship and science communication.
- [Open Science Community Network (OSCN)](https://www.oscnigeria.org/) &mdash; African-led open science network, founded in Nigeria in 2022 and part of INOSC.
- [AfricArXiv](https://info.africarxiv.org/) &mdash; preprint service for African scientists.

## Services you can hire

Africa-based companies and labs that hub members can approach for paid work &mdash;
sequencing, analysis, software or training. **Listing here is not an endorsement by the
hub**, no money changes hands for a listing, and you are expected to do your own due
diligence before commissioning anyone. If you run an Africa-based outfit serving this
community, add it yourself with a pull request.

- [BioSeq](https://www.bioseqc.com/) &mdash; Abuja. Genomics and bioinformatics: sequencing
  analysis from raw reads to interpretation, transcriptomics, single-cell, variant and
  metagenomic analysis, scientific software, and public and on-site training through BioSeq
  Academy. *Founded by Umar Ahmad, who also founded this hub &mdash; declared here so you can
  weigh it accordingly.*

## Compute and data

- [Galaxy Europe](https://usegalaxy.eu/) and [Galaxy Main](https://usegalaxy.org/) &mdash; free analysis platforms in the browser.
- [NCBI SRA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra) / [ENA](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena) &mdash; public sequence archives.
- [Bioconda](https://bioconda.github.io/) and [BioContainers](https://biocontainers.pro/) &mdash; installing tools without fighting dependencies.
- [nf-core](https://nf-co.re/) &mdash; community-curated Nextflow pipelines.
