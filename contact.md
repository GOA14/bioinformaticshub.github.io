## Day to day

The fastest route is the Slack workspace: [{{ site.slack }}]({{ site.slack_invite }}).
Post in the channel that fits your question, or in `#first-steps` if you are not sure.

## Website and membership

Anything about this site &mdash; a wrong detail on your card, a broken link, a page you
would like to add &mdash; goes through GitHub:

- [Open an issue](https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}/issues/new/choose)
- [Browse the repository](https://github.com/{{ site.repository }})
- Fork it and send a pull request: `git clone https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}.git`

We encourage community participation in running the hub, so pull requests that add
yourself to the members page, post to the blog, list an event or correct the resources
page are all welcome without asking first.

## Code of Conduct concerns

Contact the steering committee directly on Slack rather than opening a public issue.
