Bioinformatics for Nigeria’s research community

Bioinformatics Hub is an open community of scientists, students and researchers in Nigeria who analyse biological data. We share knowledge on Slack, meet online, point each other at training and opportunities, and welcome people who are completely new to the field.

What we do

  • Share knowledge

    Basic bioinformatics and NGS, RNA-Seq, ChIP-Seq, metagenomics, single-cell, microarray, pathway and network analysis.

  • Teach each other to code

    Unix command line, R and Bioconductor, Python, workflow managers, statistical computing and data visualisation.

  • Structure and drug discovery

    Molecular docking, drug design, functional and structural prediction, and systems biology.

  • Ask for help, and give it

    No question is too basic. Biologists, medical and biomedical scientists new to bioinformatics are especially welcome.

  • Circulate opportunities

    Conferences, fellowships, studentships, training schools and jobs relevant to bioinformatics in Nigeria and beyond.

  • Build the community

    Introduce yourself, find collaborators, and put your institution on the map of Nigerian bioinformatics.

Add yourself in two minutes

Membership is held in a single file in this repository, so it stays open, portable and yours.

  • 1. Fill in the form

    Open the membership issue form with any GitHub account.

  • 2. A bot opens a pull request

    Your answers are added to _data/members.yml automatically, using your GitHub username as the key.

  • 3. You appear on the site

    Once a maintainer merges, the site rebuilds and your card is live on the members page.
